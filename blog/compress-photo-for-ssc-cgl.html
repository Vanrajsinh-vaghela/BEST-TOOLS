SSC CGL Photo Size 2026: What You Need to Know

One of the biggest changes candidates should understand is that the SSC CGL 2026 application process uses a live photograph. Candidates are instructed to capture the photograph through a webcam or mobile device.

Live photo instructions

  • Use a place with good lighting.
  • Use a plain background.
  • Keep the camera at eye level.
  • Look directly toward the camera.
  • Keep your complete face inside the prescribed camera area.
  • Use a clear, full-frontal view.
  • Do not wear a cap, mask or glasses/spectacles while capturing the photograph.
  • Do not photograph an old printed photograph instead of capturing your live image.
Important: If the live photograph is poor quality, blurred, miniature or side-facing, it may cause problems with the application. Follow the live-photo instructions shown by SSC during the application process.

SSC CGL Signature Size 2026

The SSC CGL signature size 2026 requirement is particularly important because the signature must meet both file-size and image-dimension requirements.

  • Format: JPEG/JPG
  • File size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Approximate dimensions: 6.0 cm width × 2.0 cm height
  • Quality: Clear and legible
  • Orientation: Keep the signature horizontally aligned
Best practice: Do not make the signature extremely small just to meet the file-size requirement. SSC specifically warns about blurred or miniature signatures.

How to Resize SSC CGL Signature to 10 KB–20 KB

If your signature image is larger than 20 KB, you can reduce its size while keeping the signature readable. Follow these steps:

  1. Sign on clean paper: Use a clean white sheet and make your signature clear and readable.
  2. Scan or photograph the signature: Make sure the image is sharp and properly lit.
  3. Crop unnecessary white space: Remove excessive blank areas around the signature.
  4. Use JPG/JPEG format: Convert the image to JPEG/JPG if it is currently PNG or another format.
  5. Resize the image: Prepare it at approximately 6.0 cm × 2.0 cm.
  6. Compress the file: Reduce the JPEG quality gradually until the file falls between 10 KB and 20 KB.
  7. Check the final image: Make sure the signature remains clear and legible before uploading it.

Why Is My SSC Signature Rejected?

A signature may cause problems when it is too large, too small, blurred, difficult to read or saved in the wrong format.

  • File size is above 20 KB.
  • File size is below 10 KB.
  • Image is not JPEG/JPG.
  • Signature is too small or miniature.
  • Signature is blurred.
  • Too much unnecessary white space surrounds the signature.
  • Signature is not properly aligned.

How to Make SSC Signature Clear Without Increasing File Size

The easiest way is to remove unnecessary blank space before compression. A tightly cropped signature usually requires less image data than a large image containing mostly empty white background.

  1. Crop the blank area around the signature.
  2. Keep the actual signature large enough to remain readable.
  3. Convert the image to JPG/JPEG.
  4. Resize it to the approximate required dimensions.
  5. Adjust JPEG quality gradually instead of using extreme compression.
  6. Check the final file size and visual quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC CGL signature size in 2026?

The scanned signature should be in JPEG/JPG format and between 10 KB and 20 KB. The approximate image dimension is 6.0 cm width × 2.0 cm height.

What is the SSC CGL photo size for 2026?

SSC CGL 2026 uses a live photograph captured through the application module using a webcam or mobile device. Candidates should follow the live-photo instructions provided during the application.

Can I compress my SSC CGL signature to 10 KB?

Yes. You can crop unnecessary blank space, resize the signature and adjust JPEG compression until the final file is within the 10 KB to 20 KB requirement.

Can I upload a PNG signature for SSC CGL?

The SSC CGL 2026 instructions specify the scanned signature in JPEG/JPG format. Therefore, convert your signature to JPG/JPEG before uploading it.

Why is my SSC signature blurred after compression?

Excessive JPEG compression can reduce clarity. Crop the image first and then reduce the quality gradually rather than applying maximum compression. Always inspect the final signature before uploading it.

Can I use an old passport photo for SSC CGL 2026?

The SSC CGL 2026 application instructions require candidates to capture a live photograph through the application module. Do not assume that an old passport photograph can be uploaded in place of the required live capture.
