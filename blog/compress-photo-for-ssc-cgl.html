How to Compress Photo & Signature for SSC CGL Application Form

Filling out the SSC CGL application form can often be frustrating due to strict upload requirements for passport-size photographs and signatures. If your file size is slightly larger than specified, the portal throws errors like "File size should be between 20 KB and 50 KB" or "Invalid image dimensions".

Step-by-Step: How to Resize Photo to 20KB-50KB for SSC

  1. Crop the Image: Remove extra background or borders so only your face (or signature) is clearly visible.
  2. Select JPG Format: Save or convert the image to .jpg or .jpeg format (PNG files are rejected by the SSC portal).
  3. Use Client-Side Compressor: Open our Image Compressor Tool.
  4. Adjust Compression: Drag the quality slider until the output file size is strictly between 20 KB and 50 KB for photos (or 10 KB - 20 KB for signatures).
  5. Download and Upload: Download your optimized image and upload it to the SSC portal.

Important Rules for SSC Photo Uploads

  • Background: Use a light-colored or white background for maximum photo clarity.
  • Cap and Spectacles: Photographs taken wearing caps or dark glasses will lead to application rejection.
  • Signature Clarity: Always sign on clean white paper using a dark blue or black ink pen.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Why is the SSC portal rejecting my photo upload?

The most common reasons are incorrect file size (less than 20KB or more than 50KB), wrong file format (using PNG instead of JPG), or incorrect pixel dimensions.

Can I compress my signature to 10KB without blurring?

Yes. By cropping out unnecessary white space around the signature before adjusting compression, you keep the ink lines sharp while reducing overall file size.

Is it safe to compress photo online for government forms?
