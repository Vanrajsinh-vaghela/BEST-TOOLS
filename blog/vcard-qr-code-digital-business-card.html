What Is a vCard QR Code?

A vCard QR code is a QR code that contains structured contact information. Instead of asking someone to type your details manually, you can provide a single QR code that contains your contact data.

Depending on the device and scanning software, the recipient may see options to review, import, or save the contact information.

Pro Tip: Always scan your QR code yourself before printing it. Check the phone number, email address, company name and website carefully so visitors do not receive incorrect contact information.

How vCard QR Code Data Works

vCard is a structured contact-data format. A QR code can encode vCard text so compatible scanners can interpret fields such as name, phone number, email address and website.

BEGIN:VCARD VERSION:3.0 N:Smith;John;;; FN:John Smith ORG:Tech Solutions Inc TITLE:Senior Software Engineer TEL;TYPE=CELL:+1234567890 EMAIL:john.smith@example.com URL:https://example.com END:VCARD

The exact behavior after scanning depends on the QR scanner, operating system and contact application being used.

Where Can You Use a vCard QR Code?

  • Business Cards: Put a QR code on the front or back of a physical business card.
  • Resumes: Add your contact QR code to a resume or CV to make your contact information easier to access.
  • Email Signatures: Add a QR code to your professional email signature.
  • Conference Badges: Let networking contacts quickly access your professional information.
  • Brochures & Flyers: Businesses can place a contact QR code on promotional materials.
  • Freelancer Profiles: Share phone, email and portfolio details from one QR code.
Privacy Tip: A static QR code can expose whatever personal information you encode into it. Avoid adding sensitive information that you do not want printed or publicly shared.

How Large Should a Business Card QR Code Be?

There is no single size that works perfectly for every QR code because readability depends on the amount of encoded data, printing quality, contrast and scanning distance.

For a printed business card, keep the QR code large enough to scan comfortably, maintain strong contrast between the QR pattern and background, and leave sufficient blank space around the QR code.

Best Practice: Do not crop the white space around the QR code. Test the printed version from the distance at which people are expected to scan it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when someone scans a vCard QR code?

A compatible phone or QR scanner can recognize the contact information encoded in the QR code and may offer options to review, import or save the information as a contact.

Does a vCard QR code require internet access?

A static vCard QR code stores the contact information directly inside the QR code. Therefore, internet access is generally not required to read the encoded contact data. The exact save or import experience can vary by phone and scanning application.

Can I put a vCard QR code on a business card?

Yes. A vCard QR code can be printed on business cards, resumes, brochures, event badges, flyers and other networking materials.

Can a vCard QR code contain phone and email information?

Yes. Contact QR codes can include fields such as name, phone number, email, company, job title and website.

Should I test my QR code before printing it?

Yes. Always scan the QR code before printing or publishing it. Verify the phone number, email address, company name and website to make sure the encoded information is correct.
