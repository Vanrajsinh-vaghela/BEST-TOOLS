IBPS Photo Size 2026

For many IBPS applications, the photograph is required in JPG/JPEG format with a file size of approximately 20 KB to 50 KB. A commonly specified preferred image size is around 200 × 230 pixels.

Before uploading, check the relevant recruitment notification for the exact photo requirements because IBPS can update technical specifications.

IBPS photo preparation checklist

  • Use a recent, clear photograph.
  • Use a simple, suitable background.
  • Make sure the face is clearly visible.
  • Avoid blurry or heavily compressed photographs.
  • Save the image in the required JPG/JPEG format.
  • Check the final KB size before uploading.

IBPS Signature Size 2026

The commonly specified IBPS signature size is 10 KB to 20 KB in JPG/JPEG format. A preferred dimension commonly given in IBPS application guidance is 140 × 60 pixels.

Signature warning: IBPS instructions state that signatures written in CAPITAL LETTERS are not accepted. Use your normal, genuine signature.

How to prepare your IBPS signature

  • Sign clearly on clean white paper.
  • Use the ink colour specified by the relevant notification.
  • Keep the signature readable.
  • Do not use CAPITAL/BLOCK LETTERS as a substitute for your signature.
  • Crop excessive white space.
  • Convert to JPG/JPEG.
  • Compress the final file to the required KB range.

IBPS Left Thumb Impression Size 2026

The IBPS left thumb impression is another document that may be required during online application. Common IBPS guidance specifies a file size of 20 KB to 50 KB in JPG/JPEG format.

Thumb impression checklist

  • Use a clean white sheet.
  • Make a clear left thumb impression.
  • Avoid smudging.
  • Do not leave excessive empty space around the impression.
  • Scan or photograph it clearly.
  • Use the image format and dimensions specified in the current notification.

IBPS Handwritten Declaration Size 2026

The IBPS handwritten declaration is generally required as an image file in JPG/JPEG format with a commonly specified file size of 50 KB to 100 KB.

The declaration must be written in the candidate's own handwriting according to the wording and instructions provided by IBPS.

Do not type the declaration. The handwritten declaration should be genuinely handwritten and should follow the exact wording specified in the current IBPS notification.

How to Resize IBPS Photo, Signature & Documents

  1. Prepare the original image: Start with a clear photograph, signature, thumb impression or declaration.
  2. Crop unnecessary space: Remove excessive blank areas around the document.
  3. Convert to JPG/JPEG: Use the image format required by the current IBPS application.
  4. Set the dimensions: Use the exact dimensions stated in the relevant notification.
  5. Compress the image: Reduce the image quality gradually until the file enters the required KB range.
  6. Check the final file: Confirm the file size, format, dimensions and clarity before uploading.

How to Compress IBPS Signature to 10 KB–20 KB

If your signature is larger than the permitted file size, avoid applying extreme compression immediately. First crop unnecessary blank space and then reduce JPEG quality gradually.

  1. Crop blank space around the signature.
  2. Resize according to the current IBPS instructions.
  3. Convert to JPG/JPEG.
  4. Reduce image quality gradually.
  5. Stop when the file reaches 10 KB–20 KB.
  6. Open the final file and check that the signature is still clear.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBPS photo size in 2026?

IBPS application guidance commonly specifies a photograph file size of 20 KB to 50 KB, with around 200 × 230 pixels often given as the preferred size. Always verify the exact requirement for your specific IBPS recruitment.

What is the IBPS signature size in 2026?

The commonly specified signature file size is 10 KB to 20 KB in JPG/JPEG format. A preferred dimension commonly given is 140 × 60 pixels.

What is the IBPS thumb impression size?

The commonly specified left thumb impression file size is 20 KB to 50 KB. Check the current notification for the exact dimensions and other technical requirements.

What is the IBPS handwritten declaration size?

The commonly specified handwritten declaration file size is 50 KB to 100 KB. Always verify the current notification for the exact dimensions and format.

Can I use capital letters for my IBPS signature?

No. IBPS instructions state that signatures in CAPITAL LETTERS are not accepted. Use your normal signature.

Can I type the IBPS handwritten declaration?

No. The declaration is required to be handwritten by the candidate. Follow the exact wording and instructions provided in the relevant IBPS notification.

How do I reduce my IBPS photo size to 20 KB–50 KB?

Crop unnecessary background first, resize the photograph if required, convert it to JPG/JPEG and then gradually reduce image quality until the file falls within the required 20 KB–50 KB range.

How do I reduce my IBPS signature to 10 KB–20 KB?

Crop unnecessary white space, resize the image according to the current IBPS instructions and compress the JPG/JPEG file gradually until it is between 10 KB and 20 KB without making the signature blurry.
