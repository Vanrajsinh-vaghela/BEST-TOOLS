How to Compress Photo & Signature to 20KB for Govt Forms

Filling out online application forms for SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railway, or Passport can be frustrating when you keep getting the error: "File size must be between 20KB and 50KB".

Why Do Govt Forms Have Strict File Size Limits?

Government portals handle millions of applications simultaneously. To save server bandwidth and prevent crashes, these websites require small image sizes (usually 10KB to 50KB for signatures and 20KB to 100KB for passport photos).

Step-by-Step: How to Reduce Image Size to 20KB Online

  1. Crop Your Photo: Ensure your passport photo or signature image is cropped properly to remove extra white space.
  2. Use a Client-Side Compressor: Open a privacy-focused image tool that processes images locally on your device.
  3. Adjust Compression Level: Lower the quality slider until the target file size (e.g., 20KB) is reached.
  4. Download & Upload: Save the compressed image and upload it to your application form.

Tips for Perfect Passport Photo & Signature Uploads

  • Format: Always save your file as .JPG or .JPEG, as most portals do not accept PNG files.
  • Background: Use a plain white or light background for passport photos.
  • Signature Clarity: Sign on plain white paper using a dark blue or black pen under good lighting.
