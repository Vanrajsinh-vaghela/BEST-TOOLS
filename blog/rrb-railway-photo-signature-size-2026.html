RRB NTPC Photo Size 2026

The RRB NTPC photo requirement is not necessarily identical across all recruitment cycles. For example, an earlier NTPC CEN specified a scanned colour photograph in JPEG format between 30 KB and 70 KB, while a newer NTPC CEN uses a live photograph capture process during application.

Therefore, candidates applying for RRB NTPC should not rely on an old 30–70 KB rule without checking the current CEN.

RRB Group D Photo & Signature Size 2026

For RRB Group D, check the current Centralised Employment Notice for the exact photo and signature upload specifications. Railway recruitment requirements can change between application cycles.

If your CEN requires a scanned photograph, prepare the image according to its stated file size, format and dimensions. If the application uses live photograph capture, follow the camera instructions shown during registration.

RRB ALP Photo & Signature Size 2026

Candidates applying for RRB Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) should follow the photo and signature specifications in the applicable ALP CEN rather than using dimensions copied from another Railway recruitment.

For some recent RRB recruitments, the signature requirements include JPG/JPEG format, black ink on white paper, running handwriting and a specified file-size range.

RRB Technician Photo & Signature Requirements

Recent RRB Technician recruitment instructions specify a scanned signature in JPG/JPEG format with a file size of 30 KB to 49 KB and minimum image dimensions of 140 × 60 pixels. The signature should be scanned at a minimum of 100 DPI.

The exact requirement should still be checked against the CEN for the recruitment you are applying for.

RRB Signature Rules 2026

The signature is one of the most important documents in the RRB application. Recent RRB notices specify several conditions for an acceptable signature.

  • Use your own signature: The signature must be written by the candidate.
  • Black ink: Recent CEN instructions specify black ink on white paper.
  • Running handwriting: The signature should be in running/cursive handwriting.
  • No CAPITAL letters: Do not write the signature in block or capital letters.
  • Clear image: The complete signature should be visible, legible and not blurry.
  • Correct format: Use JPG/JPEG when specified by the CEN.
  • Correct file size: Stay within the exact KB range stated in your recruitment notice.
Important: Recent RRB instructions also state that the signature used in the application should match the signature used during later recruitment stages such as the examination, document verification, medical examination and appointment.

RRB Live Photograph Rules

Some recent RRB applications do not ask candidates to upload an old passport photograph. Instead, the application module captures a live photograph using a webcam or the front camera of a mobile phone.

  • Position the camera approximately at eye level.
  • Look directly at the camera.
  • Keep your entire face visible and centred.
  • Maintain a neutral facial expression.
  • Do not wear a cap or mask.
  • Do not wear glasses/spectacles when prohibited by the applicable CEN.
  • Follow the clothing and background instructions shown on the application.
  • Do not photograph an old printed or digital photograph.

How to Resize RRB Signature to 30–49 KB

If your current RRB CEN specifies a 30 KB to 49 KB signature, you can prepare the image in the following order:

  1. Write your signature in running handwriting using the required ink.
  2. Scan the signature at the required resolution.
  3. Crop excessive blank space without cutting any part of the signature.
  4. Save the image as JPG/JPEG.
  5. Resize it according to the dimensions specified by your CEN.
  6. Compress the image until it falls between 30 KB and 49 KB.
  7. Open the final file and make sure the signature remains sharp and fully visible.

Common RRB Photo & Signature Rejection Reasons

  • Wrong file size.
  • Wrong image format.
  • Incorrect dimensions.
  • Blurry or poor-resolution image.
  • Signature is incomplete or partially visible.
  • Signature written in CAPITAL/BLOCK letters.
  • Signature is not in running handwriting.
  • Non-white background for a signature where white is required.
  • Wrong ink colour.
  • Uploading a thumb impression instead of the signature.
  • Uploading an old/printed photograph where a live photograph is required.
  • Wearing prohibited items during live photo capture.

How to Check Your RRB Image Before Uploading

  1. Open the final JPG/JPEG file.
  2. Check the file size in KB.
  3. Check the pixel dimensions.
  4. Confirm the image is clear.
  5. Confirm the complete signature is visible.
  6. Confirm the file format.
  7. Compare everything with your exact CEN.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RRB photo size limit in 2026?

There is no single universal 2026 photo rule for every RRB recruitment. Some notices have specified 30 KB to 70 KB JPEG photographs, while newer application systems may use live photograph capture. Always follow your exact CEN.

What is the RRB signature size in 2026?

The requirement depends on the recruitment. Some notices specify 30 KB to 70 KB, while certain recent 2025 CENs specify 30 KB to 49 KB. Check the exact notification before uploading.

What are the RRB signature dimensions?

Certain recent RRB CENs specify minimum dimensions of 140 × 60 pixels. However, candidates should use the dimensions and application box specified by their own CEN.

Can I upload a selfie for RRB?

Do not upload a selfie unless the current application specifically instructs you to capture a live photograph using the application camera. Follow the exact live-photo instructions shown on the RRB portal.

Can I use blue ink for RRB signature?

Recent RRB CENs specify a black ink pen on white paper for the candidate signature. Follow the ink requirement in your current CEN.

Can I write my RRB signature in capital letters?

No. Recent RRB instructions specifically reject signatures written in BLOCK or CAPITAL letters. Use running handwriting.

How do I reduce an RRB signature to 30 KB?

First crop unnecessary blank space, then resize if required and reduce JPEG quality gradually. Do not compress so heavily that the signature becomes blurry or unreadable.
