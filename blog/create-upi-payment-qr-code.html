What Is a UPI Payment QR Code?

A UPI payment QR code is a machine-readable QR code containing payment information. When a customer scans it with a compatible UPI app, the app can open a payment screen with information such as the recipient's UPI ID and name.

The customer should always verify the recipient details shown in the payment app before confirming a transaction.

Common UPI Payment URI Format

A commonly used UPI payment URI can contain the payee's UPI ID, payee name, currency, and optionally a pre-filled amount.

upi://pay?pa=YOUR_UPI_ID&pn=YOUR_NAME&cu=INR

Example:

upi://pay?pa=example@upi&pn=Example%20Store&cu=INR

For a QR with a pre-filled amount, an am parameter can be included:

upi://pay?pa=example@upi&pn=Example%20Store&am=250.00&cu=INR
Important: The examples above are placeholders. Replace them with your own valid UPI information. Do not publish someone else's UPI ID or payment details without permission.

UPI QR Code Parameters Explained

Parameter Meaning Example
pa Payee UPI ID / Virtual Payment Address example@upi
pn Payee or merchant name shown to the payer Example Store
am Optional pre-filled payment amount 250.00
cu Currency code INR

How to Create a UPI QR Code for Your Shop

  1. Find your active UPI ID: Open your preferred UPI app and copy the UPI ID associated with the account where you want to receive payments.
  2. Create the payment information: Use your UPI ID and payee name to create a compatible payment URI.
  3. Generate the QR: Paste the payment URI into the BestTools QR Code Generator and create your QR image.
  4. Scan and test it: Scan the QR with a compatible UPI app and check the recipient name and UPI ID carefully.
  5. Print and display: Once verified, print the QR code and place it somewhere customers can easily scan it.

Static QR vs Fixed-Amount QR

The main difference is whether the payment amount is already included in the QR data.

Type Amount Best For
Reusable QR Customer enters the amount Shops, counters, general payments
Fixed-Amount QR Amount can be pre-filled Invoices, fixed-price services, specific bills

How to Verify a UPI QR Code Before Printing

Always test your QR code before putting it on a counter, invoice, poster, or website.

  • Scan the QR with a compatible UPI app.
  • Check that the displayed recipient name is correct.
  • Verify the UPI ID or payment destination carefully.
  • If an amount is pre-filled, confirm that the amount is correct.
  • Never approve a payment just because a QR code looks genuine.

Important UPI QR Code Safety Tips

  • Remember: scanning a QR code is generally used to initiate or open a payment flow; it does not by itself prove that the recipient is legitimate.
  • Verify the recipient: always check the recipient name and payment details shown by your UPI app before confirming.
  • Protect your UPI PIN: never share your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN, or banking credentials with anyone.
  • Check printed QR codes: for physical payment stands, make sure another sticker or QR has not been placed over your original QR.
  • Use your own payment details: don't generate a QR using an unknown person's UPI ID.

Can a UPI QR Code Be Used on an Invoice?

Yes. A UPI payment QR can be placed on invoices, bills, receipts, websites, digital menus, and payment instructions. If the payment flow supports a pre-filled amount, an amount parameter can also be included.

For invoices, always display the invoice number, total amount, and recipient details separately so the customer can cross-check the payment information.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a UPI QR code for my shop?

Use the UPI ID associated with your payment account, create compatible payment information, convert it into a QR code, and test the QR before displaying it.

Can customers use different UPI apps to scan my QR code?

A standard UPI payment QR can generally be scanned by compatible UPI-enabled payment apps. The exact payment experience can vary depending on the app, device, bank, and payment setup.

Can I add a fixed amount to a UPI QR code?

Yes. A UPI payment URI can include an am parameter such as am=250.00 to pre-fill an amount when supported by the payment app.

Does a QR code create a UPI account?

No. A QR code only represents payment information. You need an active UPI ID or payment setup to receive payments.

How do I know whether my UPI QR code works?

Scan the QR code using a compatible UPI app and confirm that the expected recipient name, UPI ID, and amount (if applicable) are displayed correctly.
