NEET Postcard Photo Size & 4×6 Photo Guide

If the current NEET instructions require a postcard-size photograph, maintain the required 4 × 6 inch aspect ratio instead of randomly cropping the image.

  • Maintain the correct 4:6 aspect ratio.
  • Do not stretch or distort the photograph.
  • Keep important text or identifying details readable if required by the current instructions.
  • Compress the image only after the dimensions and crop are correct.

NEET Photo Compression Tips

  • Crop first: Remove unnecessary blank space before compression.
  • Resize second: Set the correct dimensions or aspect ratio before reducing quality.
  • Compress gradually: Avoid extremely aggressive compression that makes the face or text difficult to see.
  • Check the final KB: Never assume the file is within the limit—check its actual size before uploading.
  • Keep the original: Save the original photograph separately in case you need to create another version.
