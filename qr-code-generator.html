Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are QR codes generated on this website free?
Yes! All static QR codes generated on BestTools are 100% free for both personal and commercial use without registration or expiration.
Do static QR codes ever expire?
No, static QR codes directly encode your information into the matrix pattern itself and will work indefinitely as long as your destination link or data remains active.
Is my data private when generating QR codes?
Yes. The QR code generation and image processing run completely client-side in your web browser using JavaScript. Your input data, Wi-Fi passwords, uploaded logos, and contact info are never saved on our servers. Standard aggregated site analytics (Google Analytics) may run separately.
Can I add a custom logo to my QR code?
Yes, you can upload your own image or brand logo to overlay in the center of the QR code. Error Correction Level H is automatically applied to maintain scan reliability.
Can I download QR codes as SVG vector files?
Yes, you can download genuine vector SVG files alongside high-resolution PNG images in 300px, 500px, 1000px, and transparent background formats.
Can I create a QR code for a Wi-Fi network?
Yes, simply select the Wi-Fi tab, enter your network name (SSID), password, and security type. Special characters are automatically escaped for seamless smartphone connection.
