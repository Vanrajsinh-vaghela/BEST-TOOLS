Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the online word counter work?
Simply type or paste your text into the box above. The tool automatically analyzes your input in real-time using native browser JavaScript.
Is my text safe and private?
Yes! Your text is processed locally in your web browser. None of your content is ever uploaded to external servers or stored in any database.
How many words are on a standard page?
A single-spaced page with 12pt font size contains roughly 500 words. A double-spaced page contains about 250 words.
What is the average reading speed?
An average adult reads approximately 200 to 250 words per minute (WPM). Our reading time calculator uses a benchmark rate of 200 WPM.
