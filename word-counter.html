How to Check Word Count in MS Word

  • Status Bar: Look at the bottom-left corner of your Microsoft Word screen.
  • Common Shortcut: Press Ctrl + Shift + G (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + G (Mac) depending on version.
  • Top Menu: Go to the Review tab and click Word Count.

How to Check Word Count in Google Docs

  • Common Shortcut: Press Ctrl + Shift + C while editing your document.
  • Top Menu: Click Tools in the top menu and select Word count.
  • Display Option: Check "Display word count while typing" for live status.

How to Count Words in PDF Files

  • Open your PDF document in Adobe Reader or Web Browser.
  • Press Ctrl + A to select all text, then Ctrl + C to copy.
  • Paste the copied PDF text into our free Word Counter box above for exact stats.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the online word counter work?
Simply type or paste your text into the text box above. Our tool automatically processes your input locally in your web browser using JavaScript and displays live text metrics instantly.
Is my text stored or saved on your server?
No! Your text is processed locally inside your web browser. None of your entered text is uploaded, saved, or transmitted to external servers. Standard site traffic analytics may run separately for general aggregated statistics.
How do I check word count in Microsoft Word or Google Docs?
In Microsoft Word, view the status bar at the bottom-left corner or press Ctrl + Shift + G. In Google Docs, press Ctrl + Shift + C or navigate to Tools > Word count. Shortcuts may vary depending on OS and software version.
How many words are on a standard single or double-spaced page?
A standard single-spaced page (A4, 12pt font) contains approximately 500 words. A double-spaced page contains roughly 250 words.
What is the difference between reading time and speaking time?
Reading time is based on an average adult silent reading speed of 200 words per minute (WPM). Speaking time is based on a standard presentation speech rate of 130 WPM.
