Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why use an online image converter?
Different web platforms, design software, and publishing systems require specific formats. Converting images to PNG keeps transparency, WebP reduces file size for web speed, and JPG ensures maximum compatibility across all devices.
Are my uploaded photos safe and private?
Yes! All image processing happens 100% client-side in your web browser using JavaScript HTML5 Canvas. Your files are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server.
Can I convert multiple images at once?
Yes, you can drag and drop or select dozens of JPG, PNG, WebP, or BMP files at once, choose your target format, and download all converted images in a single ZIP file.
How does converting transparent PNG to JPG work?
Because JPG format does not support alpha-channel transparency, transparent background areas are automatically filled with a clean white canvas background during conversion.
