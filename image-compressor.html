Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does online image compression work?
Our tool uses browser HTML5 Canvas encoding to optimize image data matrices, stripping unnecessary metadata and adjusting lossy/lossless quality factors locally in your web browser.
Are my images uploaded to any server?
No! All image compression runs 100% locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your private files are never stored or transmitted to external servers. Standard aggregated site analytics (Google Analytics) may run separately.
Can I compress multiple images at once?
Yes, you can drag and drop or select dozens of JPG, PNG, WebP, or BMP files simultaneously, adjust the compression slider, and download all results in a single ZIP archive.
Will compressing images ruin their visual quality?
By default, setting compression quality between 70% and 85% significantly reduces file size in KB/MB while keeping the visual difference virtually imperceptible to the human eye.
